# Chronicle

## Session 3

- Fae remained at Whisper Hollow and tended the sacred grove.
- Through Speak with Plants, Fae learned that the forest remembered ancient wounds, iron-bearing intruders and the importance of listening before acting.
- The Great Elk arrived and judged Fae worthy of audience.
- Fae swore the Warden's Oath and was accepted as the Eighth Warden.
- Stone-Sleeper tested Fae with an acorn stewardship challenge and accepted his answer about returning offspring rather than the original seed.
- Fae and the Great Elk visited the Pool of Memory and learned the covenant was older than the Wardens themselves.
- Fae spoke for mercy and healing rather than simple containment; the Wardens answered by recognising that the covenant would seek to heal.
- Sky-Eye revealed the Grey Order had not yet entered the deep forest, giving Fae time to prepare.
- The forest's orc clan was revealed not to be inherently hostile; one of their shamans has spoken respectfully with Old Bramble.
- Fae returned to Whisper Hollow.
- Fae advanced to level 8 and learned to take flying beast forms after meeting Sky-Eye.
