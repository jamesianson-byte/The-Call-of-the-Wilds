# NPCs

## Great Elk
- Ancient Warden of the forest.
- Accepted Fae as the Eighth Warden.
- Sees the forest as a whole and judges by service rather than title.
- Currently supportive and expects Fae to help prepare the forest for what comes next.

## Stone-Sleeper
- Ancient stone guardian of the covenant.
- Tested Fae with the acorn stewardship challenge.
- Accepted Fae's answer about returning offspring rather than the original seed.
- Respects Fae's understanding of stewardship and patience.

## Sky-Eye
- Ancient eagle Warden of the skies.
- Accepted Fae after testing his ability to see consequences rather than simply events.
- Taught Fae to think in patterns and systems across the forest.
- Now regards Fae as a trusted Warden-in-training for aerial observation and vigilance.

## Forest Oracle
- Hidden seer of the forest.
- First revealed the history of the Seven Wardens.
- Remains hidden.

## Old Bramble
- Ancient guardian and ally.
- Spoken with respectfully by the orc shaman in the forest.
- Continues to be a living witness to the old ways.

## Grey Antlers
- Known by reputation.
- Not yet met.
- Likely holds another fragment of the covenant.

## Aelthiel
- Ally and guide.
- Helped steer Fae toward Whisper Hollow.

## Harlan
- Defeated expedition leader.
- His immediate threat is over, but Grey Ledger's wider plans remain unresolved.
