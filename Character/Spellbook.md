# Fae's Spellbook

> *"Magic is not a weapon. It is another language through which the world speaks."*

---

# Spellcasting

**Class:** Druid (Circle of the Moon)

**Level:** 8

**Spellcasting Ability:** Wisdom

**Spell Save DC:** 16

**Spell Attack Bonus:** +8

**Prepared Spells:** 13

Prepared spells may be changed after every Long Rest.

# Cantrips

## Guidance

A brief blessing that aids another's efforts.

**Typical Uses**

- Exploration
- Investigation
- Survival
- Social encounters

---

## Thorn Whip

A living vine lashes outward to pull enemies closer.

**Typical Uses**

- Battlefield control
- Pulling enemies from cover
- Moving creatures into hazards

---

## Druidcraft

Minor natural magic.

**Typical Uses**

- Predicting weather
- Lighting or extinguishing small flames
- Creating harmless natural effects
- Encouraging plants to bloom

---

# Prepared Spells

## 1st Level

### Healing Word

Emergency healing at range.

Prepared almost every day.

---

### Cure Wounds

Powerful healing through direct touch.

---

### Entangle

Roots and vines restrain enemies and reshape the battlefield.

Usually cast before entering Wild Shape.

---

## 2nd Level

### Pass without Trace

The perfect spell for travelling unseen through the wilderness.

---

### Lesser Restoration

Removes disease, poison and other debilitating conditions.

---

### Moonbeam

Radiant magic against dangerous or unnatural foes.

Excellent against shapechangers.

---

## 3rd Level

### Revivify

Returns a creature to life if cast within one minute of death.

A spell Fae hopes never to need.

---

### Plant Growth

Restores damaged land and encourages healthy forests.

One of Fae's favourite spells.

---

### Speak with Plants

Allows meaningful conversation with trees, flowers, roots and other vegetation.

Has already proven invaluable during the Forest Oracle adventure.

---

## 4th Level

### Guardian of Nature

Fae temporarily embodies the spirit of the ancient wild.

**Great Tree Form**

- Advantage on Constitution saving throws.
- The ground around Fae becomes difficult terrain for enemies.
- His connection to the forest deepens, making him extraordinarily resilient.

Reserved for moments when the balance of nature itself must be defended.

---

### Freedom of Movement

A practical protection when the battlefield becomes tangled, submerged, restrained or otherwise inconvenient.

# Campaign Feature

## Voice of the Wild

While transformed using Wild Shape, Fae can communicate naturally with Beasts without expending a spell slot.

This communication is instinctive rather than verbal, conveying emotions, intentions and simple concepts.

This feature only functions while Fae is in Beast Shape.

---

# Spell Philosophy

Fae prepares magic to preserve balance rather than dominate others.

His spells reflect three guiding principles:

- Protect life whenever possible.
- Shape the battlefield before resorting to violence.
- Leave the natural world healthier than he found it.

Every dawn brings new choices, and Fae adjusts his prepared spells to suit the journey ahead.
