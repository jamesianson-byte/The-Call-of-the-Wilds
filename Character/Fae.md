# Appearance

Fae is a lean Wood Elf whose years wandering beneath ancient canopies have left him weathered rather than worn.

His long chestnut hair is loosely tied behind his shoulders, often threaded with leaves, feathers or small flowers gathered absentmindedly along his travels. His green eyes rarely settle for long, constantly observing the movement of birds overhead or listening to sounds others overlook.

His clothing is practical rather than ceremonial. A weatherproof green cloak hangs over supple leather armour, while sturdy boots have carried him across countless miles of woodland trails, mountains and rivers. A carved ash walking staff accompanies him everywhere, more as a travelling companion than a weapon.

Animals rarely fear his presence. Birds perch nearby. Deer pause rather than flee. Even wary predators often watch before deciding whether to leave.

---

# Personality

Fae believes every creature has a place within the balance of nature.

He is patient, thoughtful and endlessly curious. Rather than judging quickly, he prefers to observe, understand and learn.

He travels not because he seeks glory, but because the world is too beautiful to remain in one place forever.

While naturally peaceful, Fae understands that nature itself is not gentle. Storms destroy forests so new life can grow. Wolves hunt so their cubs survive. When violence becomes necessary, he accepts it without anger or cruelty.

His greatest joy comes from discovering somewhere—or something—he has never seen before.

## Campaign Features

### Voice of the Wild *(Campaign Feature)*

Through his deep bond with the ancient guardians of the forest, Fae can communicate naturally with Beasts while in Wild Shape.

This communication conveys instinct, emotion, scent and simple concepts rather than spoken language.

This ability is not magical, requires no spell slot and does not compel creatures to cooperate.

---

### Friend of the Ancient Forest *(Campaign Feature)*

Fae is recognised by many ancient guardians, fey and awakened creatures as a trusted protector of the forest.

While this grants no automatic friendship, those who recognise this bond will often choose conversation before conflict.

# Character Philosophy

Fae is not a hero seeking fame, riches or titles.

He is a traveller.

A student of the wild.

A healer when kindness is needed.

A guardian when strength is required.

Every road offers another lesson.

Every forest another story.

Every creature another opportunity to better understand the living world.

As his journey continues, Fae is becoming something more than a wandering druid.

He is slowly earning his place among the ancient guardians who have watched over the wilds for centuries.
